Gilda Manfrin explores music as another way of giving shape to thoughts, experiences, and feelings — intrigued by repetitions, memories, and sensuality.

Recent projects include performances and contributions at Fondazione Sozzani (Lessico Familiare), MilanOltre Festival (LSDP by Gianmarco Porru), ADI Museum, and SPRINT Independent Publishers Fair.

Her practice moves between private study, solo composition, and long-term research in sound.

MA in Sound Art, University of the Arts London.
